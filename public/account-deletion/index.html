Delete your Well-Vie account

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Well-Vie is a program of McKenzie Cusick Hair LLC. You can permanently delete your Well-Vie account directly inside the iOS app or browser member app. You do not need to contact us first. This control remains available if your account has founder or administrator access, including if it is the final founder account.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Well-Vie and sign in with your invited email.
  2. Open My profile from the navigation menu or More tab. Some app versions call this Your studio.
  3. Find Delete my account in your profile and select it.
  4. Read the warning and complete the confirmation shown. The browser version asks you to type DELETE before confirming.

Deletion begins immediately after confirmation. It cannot be undone. The app clears the locally cached sign-in session only after the deletion response succeeds or the service confirms that your membership no longer exists. If membership still exists or the app cannot confirm its removal, the app keeps you signed in so you can retry.

What deletion removes

Successful account deletion permanently removes the following from the active Well-Vie service:

  • Your Well-Vie application account and member access. Deletion of the linked Clerk sign-in account is queued for server processing; member access is removed without waiting for that background task.
  • Your membership profile, including your name, profile text and prompt answers, visibility choice, program path, welcome status, role, Circle moderation state and published snapshot, timestamps, and profile-photo reference.
  • Your uploaded profile photos from private file storage.
  • Your invitation record, including its email and invited name.
  • Your private journal entries, intentions, check-ins, practice-completion history, and Reset intake response.
  • Your active block relationships, reports you submitted, and reports about your profile in the active application database.

Your authored Circle messages are scrubbed and anonymized. Unreferenced chat attachments become eligible for server cleanup after 24 hours; cleanup can take longer during an outage. Shared report evidence may be retained where needed for a safety investigation or legal obligation, as explained in the privacy policy.

Removing the account also ends access to the Circle and all member-only practices, audio, program media, gatherings, and Reset materials. Database and private-storage membership checks stop access when the membership profile is removed. A short-lived signed media link created before deletion may continue to work only until that link expires.

Shared founder or administrator content

If your account has founder or administrator access and it uploaded guided-practice audio or shared program media, those shared program assets are retained so the member program is not broken. Their storage ownership is disassociated from the deleted authentication user. This exception applies to shared program content, not to your profile photo or member-owned reflection data.

If you reviewed another member’s profile or report as an administrator, the moderation decision about that other member may remain for safety and audit purposes, with your deleted account no longer identified as the reviewer.

Contact mckenzie@well-vie.com if shared content associated with a departing founder or administrator also needs to be reviewed or removed.

If deletion does not finish

The server removes personal profile photos before deleting the application account. If the deletion response is interrupted, the app checks your current membership. If the service confirms that your membership no longer exists, the app treats deletion as complete and clears the locally cached sign-in session. If membership still exists or the app cannot confirm its removal, the app reports that deletion did not finish and keeps you signed in so you can retry. Some personal media may already have been removed. Retry Delete my account while signed in. The operation is designed to be safely retried.

If you cannot sign in or repeated attempts fail, email mckenzie@well-vie.com from the address connected to your account when possible, with the subject “Well-Vie account deletion.” Include only the information needed to identify the account. We will verify the request and help complete deletion.

Can’t sign in or still seeing an error?

Email mckenzie@well-vie.com

Backups, logs, and required records

Deletion removes the account and associated member-owned data from the active application systems. Limited copies may remain temporarily in encrypted or access-restricted backups, security records, and system logs until normal rotation or expiration schedules complete. Backups are kept for disaster recovery rather than ordinary app use. A disaster-recovery restore may temporarily reintroduce older data; restored data remains protected and is subject to applicable deletion obligations. We may retain a minimal record longer when reasonably required by law, to resolve a dispute, prevent fraud or abuse, or enforce an agreement.

Separate website application emails, waitlist entries, program correspondence and any payment-provider records are not automatically removed by the in-app action. Ask us to review those records if you want them included in your deletion request. We explain any information we must retain and why.

Before you delete

Deletion is permanent. If you want a copy of your writing first:

  • Open Journal and use its export/share control, or
  • Open Looking back and use its export/share control.

The iOS share sheet sends the exported text only to the recipient or app you choose; the browser may download a file instead. These reflection exports do not include every category of account information. Contact us if you need a fuller data-access request before deleting your account.

The app clears its locally shared intention text when you sign out or delete the account. iOS controls when a displayed widget refreshes; remove the widget to stop showing its text immediately. Copies, downloads and screenshots you or other members saved separately cannot be recalled by account deletion.
