1. Scope

This policy covers www.well-vie.com, the member space at /app, the Well-Vie iOS app, and the related services we operate. Features differ by platform and app version. A linked meeting platform, replay host, payment service, email app or other third party also has its own privacy terms. This policy still applies to information we collect or use through those services; it does not describe their independent uses of information.

2. Information we process

We process the following categories of information, depending on how you use Well-Vie.

Account and membership information

  • Your email address, invited name, unique account identifier, invitation status, membership role, and account creation/acceptance timestamps.
  • Authentication information used for sign-in, including verification codes, session tokens, and related authentication records. Clerk provides the sign-in and verification methods enabled for Well-Vie.

Website inquiries, applications and waitlists

  • Contact and retreat inquiries can include your name, email, phone number, message, requested dates and service preferences. The contact and retreat forms open a draft in your email app; you choose whether to send it to McKenzie.
  • Reset applications include contact details, age and location, and any optional answers you choose to provide about your wellbeing, experience and support needs. These answers can reveal health information. The application form explains the optional consent choice before those questions.
  • The Ecosystem waitlist includes your name, email, country and any optional goals you share. Joining requests emails about that program; it does not subscribe you to unrelated marketing. Please do not include medical histories or other sensitive details in general inquiries or waitlist answers.
  • Application and waitlist submissions are sent through a Supabase server function and Resend to the Well-Vie team’s email inbox. They are not private journal entries. A separate confirmation email is sent to you. Where a form asks for consent, the submitted choice and notice version accompany the submission.

Profile and community information

  • The name, optional profile photo, intention, “about” text, support hopes, program path, and optional profile-prompt answers you choose to add.
  • Your choice about whether your profile is visible in the Circle member directory.
  • Circle group memberships, messages, replies, reactions, shared photos/videos/files, voice notes, sent/read timestamps, expiring typing state, and notification mute settings. An APNs device token is associated with your signed-in account when you enable notifications.
  • Circle moderation information, including content revisions, whether a profile is pending, approved, or hidden, the last approved profile snapshot, and administrator review notes and timestamps.
  • Blocks you create, and private reports you submit or that concern your profile or chat messages. A report can include its reason, your optional detail, the reported profile or message snapshot and attachments, revision, status, timestamps, and an administrator’s resolution.
  • Whether you have completed the first-welcome experience. Your light/dusk appearance preference is stored only on your device and is not sent to the Well-Vie backend.

Wellness and reflection information

  • Check-in selections, such as a feeling, a support need, or an optional custom feeling, together with the date and time of the check-in.
  • Intentions you save, practices you complete, and completion timestamps.
  • Private journal entries, including an optional title, prompt, linked practice, entry text, and timestamps.
  • Reset intake answers about what you are looking for, why now, and how you hope to be supported.

These entries may reveal information you consider sensitive, including information about your physical or emotional wellbeing. You choose what to submit.

Intention widget and device storage

On supported iOS versions, you can add an intention widget. The app saves a copy of your current intention in a shared container on that device so the widget can display it. Anyone able to see that Home Screen may see the intention. The app clears its shared copy on sign-out or account switching; iOS controls when an already displayed widget refreshes. Remove the widget if you need to stop displaying it immediately. Removing a widget does not delete your saved intention history. The browser version does not provide this native widget.

The browser member app uses cookies or similar browser storage for sign-in, session security and preferences such as appearance. The iOS app also keeps session information and temporary caches on the device. Signing out ends the local session; exports and screenshots you have saved separately remain under your control. Essential sign-in storage is needed to access the member space. We do not use advertising cookies or optional behavioral analytics on the website or in the member app.

Founder and administrator content

If you have founder or administrator access, we also process invitations and content you manage, such as guided-practice audio, program images, written practices, prompts, gatherings, replay or join links, curation choices, and associated file and edit metadata.

When you choose to record a Circle voice note, the app requests microphone permission and records the audio you send to your group. Guided recordings are program content uploaded by an authorized founder or administrator.

Information processed automatically

  • Product interactions needed to provide the service, such as check-ins, practice completions, saved content, and welcome status.
  • Routine network and technical information that Supabase or other infrastructure providers may receive when the app communicates with them, such as IP address, approximate city/country inferred from authentication IP addresses, request time, authentication and request metadata, response or error information, and basic app, browser, or device information. This information is used to deliver, secure, maintain, and troubleshoot the service.

The app does not include advertising SDKs or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs. We do not use information from Well-Vie to track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and we do not show third-party advertising.

The current app does not request your precise location, contacts, HealthKit records, advertising identifier, or payment-card details.

3. How we obtain information

We obtain information:

  • From you, when you submit a website form, join a waitlist, sign in, complete your profile or intake, check in, journal, save an intention, choose a photo, report or block a member, contact us, or otherwise use the service.
  • From an authorized Well-Vie founder or administrator, when they invite you, manage membership, or add program content.
  • Automatically from the app and service infrastructure as needed to authenticate requests, provide app features, protect the service, and diagnose failures.

4. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Verify invitations, authenticate members, maintain accounts, and provide the app.
  • Personalize recommended practices from a member’s check-in selections and display the member’s own reflection history.
  • Save journals, intentions, intake responses, profile choices, and practice progress.
  • Deliver Circle messages, check text for safety flags, review flagged or reported content and directory profiles, show approved profiles according to each member’s visibility choice, enforce blocks, investigate reports, hide content, prevent misuse, and keep a record of moderation decisions.
  • Operate Founder Studio, manage invitations and membership, schedule gatherings, and provide program practices and media.
  • Respond to requests, provide support, troubleshoot errors, protect accounts, enforce access controls, prevent misuse, and maintain service reliability.
  • Review program applications, respond to inquiries and send the program updates you requested when joining a waitlist.
  • Comply with law, respond to lawful process, establish or defend legal claims, and enforce our agreements.

We do not use private journals, intentions, check-ins, or intake responses for advertising.

Where European data-protection law applies, routine account administration and delivery of the services you request rely on performing our agreement with you or taking steps at your request before entering one. Our legitimate interests in account security, preventing abuse, troubleshooting and handling ordinary support requests are balanced against your rights. Legal obligations can require particular records or disclosures. Requested waitlist updates and optional application wellbeing answers rely on consent; you can withdraw it by contacting us. We do not treat accepting this policy, signing in, or a device permission alone as explicit consent to process health information. Sensitive information requires an additional applicable legal condition, separate from these general grounds.

Practice suggestions use your check-in selections to suggest content. We do not use this to make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you. Applications are reviewed by the Well-Vie team.

5. Who can see what

Well-Vie uses authenticated membership checks, database row-level access controls, private file storage, and short-lived signed links. The principal visibility boundaries are:

  • Journal entries, intentions, check-in history, and practice completions: available through the app only to the member who created them. They are not available to other members or through Founder Studio.
  • Intake responses: available to the member who submitted them and to authorized Well-Vie administrators who use them to provide the program.
  • Custom check-in feelings: authorized administrators can see aggregate word-use counts, without a member name attached, to improve curation. Founder Studio does not expose a member-by-member check-in history.
  • Profile: you can always see your own current profile. Turning on “Let the circle see me” submits a first profile or changed content for administrator review. Other current members see a profile only after a revision has been approved. If you edit an approved profile, other members may continue to see the last approved snapshot while the new revision is pending. Removing a photo immediately removes its reference from member-visible Circle data and prevents new member signed links. A member link already issued may remain usable until its short expiration; the current iOS app requests no more than five minutes. If visibility is off or an administrator hides the profile, other members do not see it or receive a new signed link. Authorized administrators can access current and previously approved profile information to operate and protect the service.
  • Reports and blocks: a member you block cannot see you in the member directory, and you cannot see that member there; you can review and undo your blocks. Blocks also hide each member’s messages, attachments, reactions and presence from the other in shared circle groups. Reports are available to authorized administrators for safety review. The reported member is not shown who submitted the report, and the reporter is not given access to private moderation records or another member’s account information.
  • Circle group chat: Current members explicitly added to a circle can access approved messages and shared files in that group through the app, subject to blocks. New members can see earlier approved group history. Normal member messages and edits appear immediately. Server-side text checks may hold flagged text for administrator review; the author can see their own pending messages. Authorized administrators can review pending or reported messages and attachments, including saved report snapshots. Administrator-authored posts publish directly. Group names and member display names are shared within that circle. The profile-directory visibility setting does not hide your name or messages in a group you belong to. Removing a member ends future server access; copies or screenshots already made cannot be recalled.
  • Program content: current members can access active practices, guided audio, Reset materials, gatherings, and other shared program content. Authorized administrators manage that content.

These controls do not make Well-Vie end-to-end encrypted. Well-Vie personnel and infrastructure providers with privileged system access may be able to access information when reasonably necessary for security, maintenance, support, legal compliance, or account deletion. Access should be limited to authorized purposes.

A signed file link that was created while an account had access may remain usable until its short expiration, even if membership is removed during that period.

Any recording should be explained before it starts. Do not assume a group session or Circle conversation is private to McKenzie. Legal, safety, and organizational disclosures

We may disclose information when we reasonably believe it is required by law or valid legal process; necessary to protect a person, rights, safety, accounts, or the service; or needed to investigate fraud or misuse. If ownership or operation of Well-Vie changes through a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, information may be transferred subject to appropriate confidentiality and continued notice of applicable privacy practices.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising or targeted advertising.

7. Retention

We retain account and member-created information while the account is active, unless you delete particular content or ask us to delete the account. A journal entry you delete is removed from the active application database. Pending invitation information is retained until the invitation is accepted, revoked, or otherwise no longer needed. Shared program content is retained until an authorized administrator removes or replaces it or it is no longer needed for the program.

Website inquiries and applications are held in the team’s email records while we handle the inquiry or application and any resulting program relationship, and as needed for disputes or legal obligations. Waitlist details are used until you withdraw, the relevant opening and follow-up are complete, or the list is no longer needed. Contact us to withdraw or request deletion. Deleting a member account does not automatically erase separate application emails, payment records held by a payment provider, or copies other members saved; include those records in a request if you want us to review them. We retain only necessary evidence of a consent choice, withdrawal or privacy request where needed to demonstrate how it was handled.

Block records remain while a block is active. We retain reports, reported profile and message snapshots, moderation history, and related private evidence for as long as reasonably necessary to review safety concerns, enforce the program’s standards, prevent misuse, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. A profile photo you remove or replace, or a chat attachment needed for an open report, can remain in access-restricted storage for those purposes after it is no longer visible to other members. The applicable period can depend on the report, its status, safety needs, provider schedules, and legal requirements. Access is restricted to authorized purposes.

If an administrator deletes an account, moderation decisions that administrator made about other members may remain with the deleted administrator’s identifier removed.

When account deletion succeeds, the active account and associated member-owned application data are permanently removed through the in-app Delete my account action described under Your choices and privacy rights. Limited copies may remain temporarily in encrypted or access-restricted backups, security records, and system logs until the applicable rotation or expiration schedule completes. Backups are kept for disaster recovery rather than ordinary app use. A disaster-recovery restore may temporarily reintroduce older data; restored data remains protected and is subject to applicable deletion obligations. Retention periods for those operational records vary with the record, security need, provider schedule, and legal obligation. We may retain a minimal record longer when reasonably necessary to comply with law, resolve a dispute, prevent fraud or abuse, or enforce an agreement.

Deleting a chat message removes its text and attachment reference from the conversation. Account deletion scrubs and anonymizes that account’s authored group messages. Unreferenced chat attachments become eligible for worker cleanup after 24 hours; cleanup may take longer during an outage. In the iOS app, unsent messages are stored on your device with iOS file protection, excluded from backups, scoped to the signed-in account and circle, and cleared on explicit sign-out or confirmed loss of circle access.

8. Security

We use safeguards appropriate to the nature of the service, including HTTPS/TLS transmission, Clerk authentication, membership checks, database row-level access controls, private storage buckets, short-lived signed media links, and server-only credentials for privileged account operations. Access by members and administrators is limited according to role and purpose.

No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Please protect access to your email account and device, open sign-in links only on a device you trust, and contact us if you suspect unauthorized access.

9. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawal of consent, and to appeal a decision about a privacy request. These rights may be subject to legal exceptions.

The app already provides several direct controls:

  • Edit your profile and Circle visibility from My profile (called Your studio in some app versions).
  • Privately report or block a member from that member’s Circle safety menu, and review or undo your blocks from your profile.
  • Delete individual journal entries from Journal.
  • Export your journal or “Looking back” history with its export/share control. These exports cover reflection records, not every category of account information.
  • Permanently delete your account using Delete my account in your profile. The browser version and iOS app both offer this control. See Account deletion.

To make another privacy request, email mckenzie@well-vie.com. Please write from the email connected to your Well-Vie account when possible. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request. We will not discriminate against you for exercising an applicable privacy right.

You may request a copy of all personal information we hold about you, including categories not included in the reflection export, or ask for correction, erasure, restriction or applicable portability. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests and to direct marketing. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal; a feature that needs the withdrawn processing may no longer be available, but withdrawal does not itself require closing your whole account. Tell us which processing or records your request concerns. Do not send a passport or other identity document unless we explain why a proportionate verification step is needed.

For requests covered by EU/EEA data-protection law, we respond without undue delay and ordinarily within one month. If a permitted extension is needed because a request is complex or there are multiple requests, we explain the reason within that first month; the extension can be up to two further months. We explain any refusal and available remedies. You can complain to a data-protection supervisory authority, including in the EU/EEA country where you live or work or where you believe an infringement occurred. You do not have to contact us first. Authority contact details are available from the European Data Protection Board.

10. Children

Well-Vie is an invitation-only wellness program intended for adults aged 18 and over. Public Reset applications ask applicants to confirm adult eligibility. An App Store age rating is a content rating, not permission for a child to join the program. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it as appropriate.

11. Wellness is not medical care

Well-Vie provides general wellness practices, reflection tools, and community support. It does not provide medical care, mental-health treatment, diagnosis, emergency help, or crisis services, and it is not a substitute for care from a qualified professional. Stop any practice that feels uncomfortable and seek appropriate professional or emergency help when you need it. Do not use the app to communicate an emergency.

12. Processing locations

Well-Vie is operated from the United States. Our providers and authorized service personnel may process information in the United States, India and other countries where they operate. Privacy laws and government-access rules may differ from those where you live. European data is not necessarily stored only in Europe. Where European transfer rules apply, an applicable adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses with any necessary additional measures, are required. You can contact us for information about the arrangements applicable to your data and how to obtain a copy of relevant safeguards.
